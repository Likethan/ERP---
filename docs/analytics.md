# CampusOS Analytics Engine

## Overview
The CampusOS Analytics Engine computes high-performance aggregations and cross-domain institutional performance indicators. It powers institutional executive dashboards, departmental review decks, and the Step 13 Intelligence Assistant.

## Key Capabilities
- **Date Range Presets**: Dynamic resolution of `today`, `yesterday`, `this_week`, `this_month`, `last_30_days`, `last_90_days`, and explicit custom ranges.
- **Academic Period Filtering**: Built-in support for filtering by Academic Year, Curriculum Semester, Degree Program, and Section.
- **Normalized Multi-Modal Responses**: Standardized outputs comprising chart labels, series datasets with curated hex colors, tabular headers and rows, and narrative summaries.
- **Source Provenance**: Every analytics response returns complete source provenance (dataset name, active measures, applied filters, reporting period label, generated timestamp, data as of timestamp, tenant ID).
